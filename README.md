# Antherra

The investigation workbench of The Student Statesman. You are seeing this page because you were invited. Please do not pass the files on.

## Download

| Your computer | Click to download |
|---|---|
| **Mac** (Apple chip or Intel, macOS 12 or later) | **[Antherra for Mac](https://github.com/Antherra/downloads/raw/main/Antherra-Mac.dmg)** (20 MB) |
| **Windows** (64-bit, Windows 10 or later) | **[Antherra for Windows](https://github.com/Antherra/downloads/raw/main/Antherra-Windows.zip)** (10 MB) |

Nothing else needs installing. If a link says "not found", sign in to GitHub with the account that was invited, accept the invitation from your email or [this page](https://github.com/Antherra/downloads/invitations), and click again.

Use the links above, not the green **Code** button.

## Install on a Mac

1. Open the downloaded file and drag **Antherra** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Antherra from Applications. macOS says it cannot verify the developer, because Antherra is not registered with Apple. Choose **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Antherra, and choose **Open Anyway**. You do this once.

## Install on Windows

1. Right-click the downloaded file and choose **Extract All**. Pick somewhere with a short path, such as Documents.
2. Open the folder and double-click **Antherra.bat**. If Windows warns about an unknown publisher, choose **More info > Run anyway**.
3. Antherra opens in your browser. Keep the small black window open while you work, and close it to stop.

## Check your download

Because you are asked to click past a warning, first check that the file is the real one. Compare its SHA-256 code with the one sent to you by the person who invited you, not only with the [list here](https://github.com/Antherra/downloads/blob/main/SHA256SUMS.txt).

- **Mac:** in Terminal, type `shasum -a 256 `, drag the file onto the window, and press Return.
- **Windows:** in PowerShell, type `Get-FileHash `, drag the file onto the window, and press Return.

If the codes differ, do not open the file.

## First steps

After that, on the front page choose **Load the sample case** to look around, then open **Guide**. The **?** button explains whichever tool you are on.

The first time, you choose a passphrase and are given a recovery code. Write the code down and keep it away from your computer: if you lose both, nobody can open your cases. Your cases stay on your computer, encrypted. Keep FileVault (Mac) or device encryption (Windows) on too. Nobody else's cases or keys are in your copy.

## Updating

Download the newest file from this page and install it over the old one. Your cases and keys are kept. Coming from 1.4 or earlier, they are encrypted when you choose your passphrase.
